# Leave Policy (India)

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 8 unused days may be carried into the next year.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.

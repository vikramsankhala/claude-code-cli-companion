# Handover checklist

*An engagement is complete only when the customer's team runs it without the FDE (Chapter 29, stage 4).*

- [ ] Named owner on the customer side, accepted in writing
- [ ] Runbook: start, stop, deploy, roll back, rotate keys, common failures
- [ ] Monitoring and alerts go to the customer's on-call, and were tested
- [ ] Evaluation set and gates handed over and running in the customer's CI or schedule
- [ ] Architecture notes and ADRs in the customer's repository
- [ ] Autonomy level for each automated action agreed and recorded (shadow, assist, act with notice, act within budget)
- [ ] Training delivered; attendees listed; recording or notes stored
- [ ] All access granted to the FDE removed; shared secrets rotated
- [ ] Customer data copies outside approved locations: none (confirmed)
- [ ] Open issues list with owners and dates
- [ ] Success measures re-measured against the brief and reported to the sponsor
- [ ] Field notes submitted to the product team

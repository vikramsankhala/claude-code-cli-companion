# Retrospective (about 200 words)

- **Delegated:** which tasks ran as background agents, and for how long.
- **Corrected:** what you had to fix or rewind, and why the agent got it wrong.
- **Review:** what `/diff`, `/security-review` and the P5 reviewer caught.
- **Cost:** total from `/usage`, and whether it was worth it against doing it yourself.
- **Next time:** one thing you would plan differently.
